# Preview ledger

One row per merged change that people can see. A checker checks each once on staging after the
merge; a release waits until every row it carries has passed.

| PR | Merged | What to check, where | Checked by | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| #<n> | <date> | <screen, steps, expected> | <checker> | <pass / fail: what was seen / open> |
